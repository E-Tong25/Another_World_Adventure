Combining my love of Ancient Mythology, video games, and Isekais, I created a text-based, "choose-your-own-adventure" console application where the user enters into a new world, after being reincarnated, joins a guild, goes on quests, meets characters, fights in arenas, and shops and eats at the local tavern and shop.

# Play by Play
* The player starts the game by entering into a new world, after being reincarnated.
* The player can name themselves, which is stored and used within the program.
* The player is then directed by an omniscient being named Q, who has a snarky, loud personality.
* The player goes on quests, fights enemies, joins a guild, and so much more.

# Features Of The Project
- Master Loop
- Multiple derived classes
- Calculated and displayed data
